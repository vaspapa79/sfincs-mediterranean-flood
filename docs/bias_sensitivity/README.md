# Rainfall bias-correction sensitivity (B2)

Sensitivity of the strict EMS validation metrics to the IMERG gauge-anchor
**target_mm** (the only varied parameter; all else fixed). For each target the
IMERG event-window total is rescaled to that watershed-mean, the model is rebuilt
and re-run, and strict metrics (h>0.10 m on the canonical mask = msk>0 ∩ EMS AOI
union) are recomputed. Generated by `scripts/bias_sweep.py`
(`validation/validate_event_v2.py` for scoring); baseline = the canonical run,
re-validated to docs/AUDIT.md **before** sweeping and the canonical model re-checked
**after** (both 0.106/0.873 Mandra, 0.456/0.947 Pineios — untouched). SFINCS was
re-run for the non-baseline targets only (isolated model dir; canonical outputs
never overwritten).

## Mandra (EMSR257) — calibrated 200 mm (×8.55)
| target (mm) | ×mult | CSI | HR | FAR | B | GSS |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 95  | 4.06  | 0.104 | 0.754 | 0.892 | 6.99 | 0.027 |
| 140 | 5.99  | 0.106 | 0.824 | 0.891 | 7.58 | 0.028 |
| **200** | **8.55** | **0.106** | **0.873** | **0.892** | **8.11** | **0.028** |
| 255 | 10.90 | 0.104 | 0.893 | 0.894 | 8.46 | 0.025 |

CSI is **flat within ±0.001** across a 2.7× rainfall range; HR rises monotonically
(0.754→0.893) while FAR stays ~0.89 (over-prediction-dominated, B≈7–8.5). The
calibrated 200 mm sits on a CSI plateau — the result is not sensitive to the exact
target and the calibration is not CSI-tuned.

## Pineios (EMSR692) — calibrated 400 mm (×2.73)
| target (mm) | ×mult | CSI | HR | FAR | B | GSS |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 220 | 1.50 | 0.475 | 0.902 | 0.499 | 1.80 | 0.239 |
| 295 | 2.01 | 0.466 | 0.926 | 0.516 | 1.91 | 0.221 |
| **400** | **2.73** | **0.456** | **0.947** | **0.532** | **2.02** | **0.201** |
| 510 | 3.47 | 0.446 | 0.959 | 0.545 | 2.11 | 0.184 |

A precision/recall trade-off: more rain raises HR (0.902→0.959) but lowers CSI/GSS
(FAR climbs). CSI varies only ~0.03 across a 2.3× range. The calibrated 400 mm is
gauge-anchored (Dimitriou et al. 2024), **not** CSI-maximising (220 mm would give a
marginally higher CSI) — the honest, defensible choice, and it gives the best HR.

## Files
- `{event}.csv` — metrics per target (target_mm, multiplier, is_baseline, CSI/HR/FAR/B/GSS)
- `{event}_sensitivity.png` — CSI & HR vs target_mm, calibrated value marked
- `{event}_rain_total_{T}mm.{png,nc}` — corrected IMERG event-total field over the
  catchment at baseline + the two extremes. The spatial **pattern** is the IMERG
  field (uniformly rescaled by the multiplier over the event window); peak cell
  totals: Mandra 170 / 357 / 455 mm (95/200/255), Pineios 479 / 871 / 1111 mm
  (220/400/510) — physically plausible convective/orographic maxima at the
  calibrated value.

**Takeaway:** both headline results are robust to the bias target across a wide
range (CSI flat for Mandra, ±0.03 for Pineios), and the calibration is anchored to
independent gauge estimates rather than tuned to the validation metric.
