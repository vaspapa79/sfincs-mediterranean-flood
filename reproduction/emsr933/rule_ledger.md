## 4. R-input derivation details
- **Catchment delineation.** pyflwdir D8 on GLO-30 decimated 3× (≈ 76 × 93 m cells) over 23.4–26.4 E / 34.8–35.7 N
  (3600 × 1080 cells, 8415 km² land); sea (≤ 0 m) masked so the coast is the outlet; all depressions filled for the
  flow network (conventional delineation). Caveat: filling routes the Lasithi polje's overflow (spill 889.6 m) into
  the Aposelemis system, so the AOI01 inflow of 162.6 km² and the 441 km² catchment of the Kastelli depression
  include the 223 km² Lasithi closed basin, which in reality (and in SFINCS, which does not fill) does not drain
  overland. Per-AOI inflow candidates (lowest-z_b channel cell at the AOI edge) are in `stage1_terrain.json`
  (`aois[].external_inflow_streams_top`); none is needed because R3 is off.
- **Sea cells (R5).** `stage1_terrain.json` → `domains.{east,central,west,island}.contains_sea = true` (90-m sea
  cells: see JSON); AOI boxes 02/04/06/07/08 contain sea cells, 01/03/05 do not.
- **Closed depressions.** Lasithi (AOI03): 35.76 km² of floor below the 889.6 m spill, 1979 Mm³ storage; its 223 km²
  internal catchment receiving 0.4–0.5 m of rain yields ≈ 90–110 Mm³, so the polje cannot spill — SFINCS will pond
  all runoff in the lowest floor cells (no sinkhole drainage; the real plateau drains through the Chonos
  katavothra). Omalos (AOI07): 5.51 km² plateau floor, 125.6 Mm³ below the 1078.6 m spill, 24.8 km² catchment —
  same behaviour. These are noted, not modified (task pitfalls). The 2.43 km², 308-m-deep "depression" in the
  Omalos box is a GLO-30 artefact in the Samaria gorge (floor 238 m) — to be watched in the Stage-2 build QC.
- **GLO-30 in gorges.** The component list per AOI (`stage1_depressions.json`) is the QC checklist for DEM voids/
  artefacts inside AOIs at Stage 2.

## 5. Domain design (same way as EMSR773 / EMSR664)
Precedent (Table S4, Sect. S3): "Run-dependent numerical parameters (grid resolution, simulation window, spin-up,
and the R2 anchor total) were not part of the frozen rule; they were set by the Table 2 conventions once the domains
were drawn, before any comparison against the EMS extents." Table 2: Mandra 50 m "to resolve the urban street
geometry" (~20 km² catchment), Strymonas/Pineios 150 m "basin-scale … trade base-grid resolution for catchment
coverage"; Table S4: Valencia 100 m / 4 px ("convective urban / ravine", catchments ~50–1400 km²), Emilia 150 m / 4 px
("levee-breach, multi-catchment", ~500–5000 km²). The held-out domains were single boxes around the AOIs and their
contributing catchments (Valencia 0.95° × 0.70°, 801 × 841 cells @ 100 m; Emilia 1.20° × 0.75° @ 150 m).

- **Domain choice.** One island-wide domain (YAML) — it is the hydrological closure of all eight AOIs (every
  Cretan catchment is inside, so R3 has nothing to inject and the coast is a boundary everywhere), it is a single
  run as for the held-out events, and at 100 m its 1.60 M cells (0.71 M land) are 2.4 × Valencia's 0.67 M.
  Per-cluster domains (three Valencia-sized runs) are the alternative if the Δx decision is 50 m (6.4 M cells
  island-wide).
- **GAP-1 — Δx.** R does not fix it and the Table 2 / S4 conventions are qualitative. By archetype metadata the
  closest precedent is Valencia (convective/orographic flash floods in ravines draining tens to a few hundred km²,
  small towns; AOI catchments: largest streams 17–226 km²) → **100 m / 4 px (25 m effective)**, written into the
  YAMLs as provisional. Options for the author: **A** 100 m island (1.60 M cells); **B** 150 m island (0.71 M; the
  basin-scale convention); **C** 50 m per cluster (east 1.81 M, central 2.22 M, west 0.60 M; the Mandra convention —
  "urban street geometry" — applies to the towns only); **C′** 100 m per cluster (0.45 / 0.55 / 0.15 M). Consequence
  for the truth, reported but not used in the choice: observed-wet cells at 100 m are 443 (Tzermiado) and 3–24
  for the other four AOIs (§3).
- **GAP-2 — z_max.** The 250 m (400 m Pineios) active-mask convention would deactivate the Lasithi plateau (floor
  808 m), the Omalos plateau (1035 m), Anogeia (AOI05 terrain 119–1530 m) and the massifs that generate the
  runoff. Proposed **2500 m** (above the highest AOI terrain, 2329 m; effectively all land active → "land cells"
  column). Author decision.
- **UTM 34N.** Point scale factor k = 1.00036 (23.72 E) to 1.00216 (26.01 E), areal ≤ 1.0043: ≤ 0.22 % in length,
  ≤ 0.43 % in area at the east end (`domains.*.utm34_scale_corners`, `aois[].utm34_scale_at_centroid`). All EMS km²
  on this branch are computed in EPSG:32634 and carry the same factor; acceptable for a single-zone island model.
- **Window.** As §2: tstart 2026-09-28 00:00 → tstop 2026-10-06 00:00 (8 d), ERA5 needed through 2026-10-06 00:00
  (available ≈ 11–12 Oct; daily probe in `timing.md`).
