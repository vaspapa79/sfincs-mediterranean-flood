- **Grid** = `mmax × nmax @ dx` from `sfincs.inp`; all dx = dy; rotation 0.
- **hydromt build** = first → last timestamp in `model/hydromt.log` (HydroMT 0.10.1, hydromt_sfincs 1.2.2 in every log).
- **Forcing present** = files found in the run's `data/` (sizes/counts from `ls`/`find`); junction/hardlink seeds verified by listing through the junction.
- Env paths in every example YAML / as-run record point at the author machine (`C:/Users/vaspapa/...`); the worktree README of CODE-9 says they resolve via `skill/machine.local.yaml`.
